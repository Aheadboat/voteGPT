## Basis and limits

The [Copyright Office FAQ](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html) distinguishes unprotected facts, names, titles, and short phrases from protected expression. Its [Compendium sections 313.3(C) and 313.4(C)](https://www.copyright.gov/comp3/chap300/ch300-copyrightable-authorship.pdf) gives the same basis for facts and brief labels. Applying that distinction to independently structured candidate/office/district/status facts is an inference supporting this narrow proposal. An absent license statement is not, by itself, a reason those factual fields require a copyright grant. This does not settle unrelated privacy, access-contract, or other legal restrictions.

[California Elections Code section 8148](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=ELEC&sectionNum=8148.) requires the general-election certificate and county delivery; it does not itself impose website publication. Actual public access is supported by the SOS election-page link and its [Public Records Act FAQ](https://www.sos.ca.gov/administration/public-records-act-requests/public-records-act-frequently-asked-questions). Public access does not clear all downstream uses. The actual PDF notices and complete row inventory have not yet been inspected.
